Our Community

Our Ganesh Utsav, Our Community

Building traditions and lifelong friendships within R33 Elevate Tower.

🕉️

Our Story

It’s our very first Ganesh Utsav at R33 Elevate Tower! Since most of us have just moved in, this festival is the best chance to finally meet the people living across the hall and on other floors. We'd love to see everyone come down—join the games, help out with the pandal, attend the daily aartis, and get to know your neighbours. Let’s make our first celebration a memorable one together.

🤝

Our Team

Everything is organized entirely by residents volunteering their time. To pull this off smoothly, we need people to take ownership of specific tasks—decoration, pooja setups, crowd coordination, and games. If you've already joined the volunteer group, please speak up and pick a task! If you haven't joined yet, step forward—sharing the load makes it fun for everyone.

🎉

Get Involved

Everyone is welcome — owners and tenants alike. Whether it's decoration, event hosting, sports coordination, or simply spreading the word, there's a place for you on the team.
