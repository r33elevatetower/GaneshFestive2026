About Us

Our Ganesh Utsav, Our Community

🕉️

Our Story

Every year, residents of R33 Elevate Tower come together to bring Bappa home. What began as a small gathering has grown into a festival that unites every wing, floor and family in the tower.

🤝

Our Team

A committee of resident volunteers handles pooja arrangements, decoration, cultural events, sports and coordination — all on a purely voluntary basis, with full transparency on funds collected and spent.

🎉

Get Involved

Everyone is welcome — owners and tenants alike. Whether it's decoration, event hosting, sports coordination or simply spreading the word, there's a place for you on the team.

Contribute

How to Contribute

  • Scan and pay

    Use the UPI QR code alongside to make your contribution — any amount is welcome and appreciated.

  • Share your details

    Send your payment screenshot, name and flat number to the committee contact number below.

  • Get your receipt

    Once verified, you'll receive an official receipt and, where applicable, a registration code for events.

Scan with any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.) to contribute.
